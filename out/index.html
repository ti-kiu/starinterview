Common questions

Is this really free?↓

Yes. Practice STAR method answers and generate interview answers without creating an account or paying anything.

What is the STAR method?↓

STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, Result. It's a structured way to answer behavioral interview questions with clear, compelling stories from your experience.

How does the AI feedback work?↓

Our AI scores each component of your STAR answer and gives specific suggestions — like adding measurable results or clarifying your personal contribution.

Do I need to create an account?↓

No. Practice and generate answers instantly. Optional free account lets you save history and track progress.

Can this help if English is not my first language?↓

Yes. The AI provides language clarity suggestions alongside STAR structure feedback, built for non-native speakers.
